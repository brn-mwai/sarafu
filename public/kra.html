If KRA assesses you

KRA can raise its own assessment when a business cannot produce records. A business with no books does not avoid tax. It gets estimated. A Notice of Objection must reach the Commissioner within 30 days of service under section 51 of the Tax Procedures Act. The Commissioner must decide within 60 days or the objection is deemed allowed. An appeal to the Tax Appeals Tribunal is within 30 days of that decision, with undisputed tax paid first.
